HRA Automated Submission Tool

What this tool does

Flexible Informatics' MERP is a reimbursement plan that pays back part of what you spend toward your IBX medical deductible, plus half of your copays. The catch is that nothing happens automatically: you have to find each Explanation of Benefits (EOB) on the IBX website, download it, and file it by hand on Alera's Summit portal, one claim at a time.

This tool does the tedious part for you:

  • Fetches every claim for the plan year from IBX, for everyone on your plan, along with its EOB or Proof of Claim PDF, and keeps them on this computer.
  • Works out which EOBs are worth submitting and roughly how much you'll get back, using the plan's rules.
  • Reads Summit to see what you've already submitted and what Alera has paid, so nothing is filed twice.
  • Prefills the Summit claim form for a chosen EOB, with the PDF attached. You look it over and click Submit yourself. The tool never submits a claim on its own.

How the money works

The HRA has two separate benefits, and each EOB can be filed against one or both:

Gold Classic $2,500Silver Secure $4,750
Deductible benefit — you pay the first…$1,500 single / $3,000 family$3,750 single / $7,500 family
…then the HRA pays 100% of the next$1,000 single / $2,000 family$1,000 single / $2,000 family
Copay benefit50% of every in-network copay, up to $500 per year

Only in-network claims count. Coinsurance is never reimbursed. Deductible EOBs below the threshold are still worth submitting so Alera's running total is right. Claims must be filed within 60 days of the plan year ending.

How to use it

  1. Setup tab: enter your IBX username and password, and your Summit username and password. They save as you type and are stored encrypted on this computer only.
  2. Click Fetch claims from IBX. An Edge window opens and logs in. If IBX asks for a verification code, type it in that window; the tool waits for you. It then reads who is covered and downloads every claim with its EOB (medical) or Proof of Claim (pharmacy).
  3. Click Sync Summit submissions. Same idea: a window opens, you enter the emailed or texted code when asked. The tool reads your transaction history, matches it to the EOBs, and detects which plan you're on.
  4. The Claims tab unlocks. Each row shows the EOB, whether it qualifies, the expected payout, and a Prefill button for the deductible and/or copay part.
  5. Click Prefill, check the values, and confirm. Summit opens with the form filled in and the EOB attached. Review it, choose how you want to be paid, and click Submit Claim yourself. The tool records it and later syncs the approval and payment.
  6. Repeat whenever new EOBs arrive. Fetch only downloads what's new or changed.

Good to know

  • The Logs tab shows what each job is doing and why one failed, with a screenshot when a website didn't behave.
  • Jobs run one at a time. If one is waiting for you, a pill appears at the top right.
  • Everything lives in a folder on this computer. Delete everything on the Setup tab wipes it; nothing on IBX or Summit is changed.
